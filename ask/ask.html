Remember to...

  • Get the spellings right, especially for player names.
  • Try rephrasing if a question has us stumped, we are still learning 🙂 !
  • Use specific terms - ‘batting’, ‘bowling’, ‘in an innings’, ‘in a season’ (‘ABD batting record’, instead of ‘ABD record’)
  • Use ‘versus/vs/v’ for batsman-versus-bowler queries, and ‘and’ to compare two players (‘Kohli vs Rashid’, ‘Warner and Rohit in death’)
