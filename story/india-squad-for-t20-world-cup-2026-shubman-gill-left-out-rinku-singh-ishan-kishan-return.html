Gill left out of India's T20 World Cup squad; Rinku, Kishan make comebacks

The same squad will play the five-match T20I series against New Zealand

ESPNcricinfo staff
20-Dec-2025
1 hr ago
24
Suryakumar Yadav will lead India but there will be no Shubman Gill • AFP/Getty Images

India left out Shubman Gill from their squad for the 2026 T20 World Cup. There was no room for Jitesh Sharma either as the selectors picked Rinku Singh and Ishan Kishan in the 15-member squad. Axar Patel was named the vice-captain. The same team will play the five-match T20I series against New Zealand.

India are the defending champions, having beaten South Africa in the final of the 2024 edition.

India's squad for T20 World Cup 2026

Suryakumar Yadav (capt), Abhishek Sharma, Sanju Samson (wk), Tilak Varma, Hardik Pandya, Shivam Dube, Axar Patel (vice-capt), Rinku Singh, Jasprit Bumrah, Harshit Rana, Arshdeep Singh, Kuldeep Yadav, Varun Chakaravarthy, Washington Sundar, Ishan Kishan (wk)
