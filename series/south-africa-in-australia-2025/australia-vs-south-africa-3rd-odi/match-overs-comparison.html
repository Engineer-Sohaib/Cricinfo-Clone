Why are some overs highlighted in blue?

By default, we highlight all overs that have a significant impact on the outcome of a match. This includes:

  • Overs with 12 or more runs, 2 or more wkts
  • Maiden overs
  • Overs where win% changes by +-10% vs the previous over
  • Overs where forcasted score changes by +-10 Runs vs the last ball of last over
